# Codebook: turnover-orthodox-s1

Setting: work teams in a large multi-site manufacturing and services firm. Unit of observation: team-year. N = 1200.

All variables are standardized indices (mean ~0, SD ~1); higher values mean more of the construct.

| variable | description |
|---|---|
| `team_turnover` | Share of team members who left and were replaced during the year (standardized index) |
| `team_performance` | Supervisor-rated team performance against annual targets (standardized index) |
| `knowledge_loss` | Loss of team-specific procedural knowledge, from quarterly skills audits (standardized index) |
| `idea_novelty` | Novelty of the team's submissions to the firm's improvement program, rated by independent judges (standardized index) |
| `routine_codification` | Degree to which the team's work procedures are documented in written routines (standardized index) |
| `task_interdependence` | Degree to which members' tasks depend on one another's outputs (standardized index) |
| `hiring_quality` | Quality of replacement hires, from structured assessment scores at entry (standardized index) |
| `workload` | Average weekly workload per member relative to the firm norm (standardized index) |
| `team_size` | Number of team members (standardized index) |
| `budget_slack` | Unspent discretionary budget available to the team (standardized index) |
| `training_investment` | Hours of formal training purchased for the team (standardized index) |
