# Codebook: monitoring-contrarian-s1

Setting: operating units of a large knowledge-intensive services firm. Unit of observation: unit-year. N = 1200.

All variables are standardized indices (mean ~0, SD ~1); higher values mean more of the construct.

| variable | description |
|---|---|
| `monitoring_intensity` | Intensity of electronic performance monitoring of employees in the unit (standardized index) |
| `creative_output` | Number and quality of novel solutions and process improvements generated by the unit, judged by an expert panel (standardized index) |
| `routine_output` | Volume of standard tasks completed per employee (standardized index) |
| `perceived_autonomy` | Employees' reported discretion over how they do their work (standardized index) |
| `trust_in_management` | Employees' reported trust in unit management (standardized index) |
| `feedback_frequency` | Frequency of performance feedback conversations between supervisors and employees (standardized index) |
| `feedback_quality` | Developmental quality of feedback, rated by trained observers (standardized index) |
| `tech_investment` | Unit spending on workplace technology and analytics (standardized index) |
| `unit_budget` | Total discretionary unit budget (standardized index) |
| `wellness_program` | Scope of employee wellness programs offered by the unit (standardized index) |
| `job_tenure` | Average employee tenure in the unit (standardized index) |
| `manager_span` | Number of direct reports per supervisor (standardized index) |
