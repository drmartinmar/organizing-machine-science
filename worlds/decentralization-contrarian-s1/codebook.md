# Codebook: decentralization-contrarian-s1

Setting: operating units of multi-unit firms. Unit of observation: unit-year. N = 1200.

All variables are standardized indices (mean ~0, SD ~1); higher values mean more of the construct.

| variable | description |
|---|---|
| `decision_decentralization` | Share of operating decisions delegated from headquarters to the unit (standardized index) |
| `unit_performance` | Unit operating performance relative to plan (standardized index) |
| `environmental_turbulence` | Volatility of demand and competitive conditions in the unit's market (standardized index) |
| `local_knowledge_use` | Extent to which unit decisions draw on local customer and market information, from decision audits (standardized index) |
| `coordination_cost` | Managerial time and rework spent reconciling the unit's decisions with other units (standardized index) |
| `it_integration` | Degree of integration of the unit's information systems with headquarters systems (standardized index) |
| `unit_size` | Number of employees in the unit (standardized index) |
| `managerial_experience` | Average years of managerial experience in the unit leadership team (standardized index) |
| `hq_attention` | Attention headquarters devotes to the unit, from meeting and review logs (standardized index) |
| `formal_planning_hours` | Hours the unit spends on formal strategic planning (standardized index) |
| `market_concentration` | Concentration of the unit's market (standardized index) |
